# Art Archives

<details class="pv-reveal">
  <summary>My Story</summary>
<p>
Several years ago, my daughter, Li-Mae, told me about an internet led art program called Inktober. The idea of the program was that each day of the month, a prompt was given, and then people drew something that that prompt meant to them. The guy who started it was looking for a way to improve his own drawing skills and develop regular drawing habits.(This program grew to include thousands of artists across the internet.)  Also, part of the goal was that the art had to be shared...on the internet, on the kitchen fridge, a bathroom stall...anywhere someone else could see it. 
</p>
<p>    
"That sounds like esomething you'd be interested in, Dad, Li-Mae said to me. (She was already participating, literally drawing something on a sticky note once a week to each prompt.) So, in 2019, I started participating, too. I liked the idea of having a driving goal to practice and develop m own drawing skills. Somewhere along the way, I told other artist and artsy people about it. Beore long, we started our own little drawing group and shared our work on What's App. Instead of drawing everyday in October, we drew something once a week throughout the year. 
</p>
<p>  
Now, after 6 years of doing this, I have quite a collection of drawings. I certainly can't call it all art. Imagine a writer scribbling pages and pages that end up as crumpled paper in the trash can before coming up with a finished product. Well, in my case, there's no crumpled paper in the trash. Some of these are drawings on napkins. Some are exercises in shading, speed drawing, off-handed drawing, perspective, representative drawing, etc. Many drawings (especially the earlier ones) are based on a photo or image found somewhere on the internet As you will see, I started almost exclusively using blank ink. Later, I introduced color, and occasionally some other media like pastels or colored pencil. As I roll out each years gallery, I think you'll see some development. Take a look and see what you think. </p>

</details>
