   <h2>April 10, 2019 &mdash; Our Animal Neighbors</h2>

  <div class="poem-text">
<p>
We live in a tropical forest, so, no surprise, many animals we think of as exotic are commonplace. (On our first day here, we saw a tree full of howler monkeys on a walk to the beach.) And many farm animals share roads, yards, and driveways with the locals. Lucky for our readers, Tico Times staff photographer, Julie McClellan, has suspended her strike for better working conditions (as in, fewer bugs in the house) and has produced a stunning array of photos to highlight the variety of domestic and wild animals we share our space with here. Enjoy.
</p>
