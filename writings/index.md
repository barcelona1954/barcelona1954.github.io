# Writings

Poems, short reflections, and other written work.

<details class="pv-reveal">
  <summary>My Story</summary>
  <p>
  When I was an undergraduate in college, I took a writing class at UC Berkeley from Jim XXX, who was the driving force behind the influential teaching program, the Bay Area Writing Project. The class was an eye opener for me and liberating, as it focused more on expression and certain language tricks rather than formal grammar and argument. 
  </p>
  <p>
  One day in class, we had a short writing exercise. We had maybe 10-15 minutes, I don't remember exactly, to form an introductory paragraph based on a subject topic written on the chalkboard. 
  </p>
  <p>
  I don't remember the topic, but I do remember being nearly paralyzed by the paltry few words and simple, unfinished paragraph I was able to construct. When time was up, our task was to form small groups (there were 5 or 6), pass papers around to each group, and then each reader in each group read the papers and rated them. 
  </p>
  <p>
  After all the papers had been passed around, Jim collected the "ratings" and posted them on the chalkboard. Given the sparse number of words I was able to scribble down, I was dreaded seeing the results tabulated. I was shocked and in utter disbelief, however, that my paper was the hightest, rated in the class. 
  </p>
  <p>
  I look back on that single experience as one that made me believe in myself. I went on to write and essay or two for the college paper, tutored and taught writing, and gained confidence in myself. 
  </p>
  <p>
  Over time, I realized that I enjoy writing about the world around me and the world I've experienced. (But it's so damn hard !) That said, these experiences have takend shape here in reflective narratives and an occasional poem (created by Godric Saint). Sometimes I get serious and tackle and essay on a topic that occupies my mind. 
  </p>
</details>
